Concept prototype prepared for KFAS — not an official KFAS system. Recording a real interview needs the candidate’s consent, taken before you start.

Live recording

Record the interview, and check it as it happens

Turn the microphone on and the room is transcribed live. Every question is checked against the approved bank for the vacancy as it is asked — in the bank, outside it, or one that should not have been asked at all — and each answer is captured under the question and the criterion it belongs to. When you stop, the whole thing can be saved into the review queue for someone else to listen to.

    Before you record anyone

    • Tell the candidate they are being recorded and why, and get their consent in writing at invitation.
    • Say who will listen to it, and how long it is kept.
    • Transcription is never perfect. The recording is the record; the text is a way into it.
    • A check is a prompt to a human, not a decision. Nothing here scores a candidate.
