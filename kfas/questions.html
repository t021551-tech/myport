Question bank

Every question, tied to something it measures

A question that is not in the bank cannot be scored, because there is no criterion for the answer to land on. When one is asked anyway, it is recorded against the recording and the panellist has to account for it here.

Questions waiting to be explained

The approved bank

Propose a question

A new question joins the bank only after HR has approved it, and only against a criterion that already exists for that vacancy. That is what stops a panel inventing a question between candidates.

Retired questions

Kept visible on purpose. A retired question with its reason attached is how a panel learns why it was a problem.
