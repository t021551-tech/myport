Kuwaiti Oil Tankers

ناقلات النفط الكويتية

The Kuwait Oil Tanker Company owns and manages the ships that carry Kuwait’s crude oil, refined products and LPG. This is its fleet as a dataset — thirty hulls, the terminals they load at, the grades they lift — published as CSV, JSON and a relational schema you can load and query.

What is real here, and what is not

The vessel register, the ports and the cargo grades are real, compiled in September 2026 from KOTC’s own material, trade press and AIS-derived vessel registries. Port coordinates and sea distances are approximate, and crude assay figures are nominal.

The voyage table is synthetic. It is generated from a fixed seed so the register can be queried as a fleet rather than read as a list, and every row in it carries data_source = 'synthetic'. No row describes a real shipment. Anything on this page drawn from it is marked.

Ten of the thirty deadweight figures are carried across from a confirmed sister ship rather than published for that hull, and four vessels carry an AIS registration that disagrees with their recorded flag. Both are flagged in the data. The full account is in the data dictionary.
