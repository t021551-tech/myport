Current best

Light roast, 1:14, heavy hail, three threads of za’faran

Six brews in and this is the one I’d serve to guests. Light roast keeps it golden rather than muddy, the cardamom goes in after the boil so it stays bright, and the saffron gets ten minutes in the thermos to open up.

What I’ve learned so far

  • Ratio 1:14 is the sweet spot. Below 1:12 it turns bitter, above 1:16 it turns into tea.
  • Hail Always after the boil. Boiling it flattens the whole thing.
  • Za’faran Three threads. Never eight.
  • Rest Ten minutes in the thermos before pouring — it settles and rounds out.
