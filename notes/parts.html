Considered and rejected

  • No Lowering springs — wrong car, wrong roads, and speed bumps would end me.
  • No Loud exhaust — sounds great for a week, then it’s just your daily drive being loud.
  • No Cold air intake — the dyno numbers people quote are noise, and it drinks dust here.
  • No 20" wheels — harsher ride, dearer tyres, more kerb anxiety. Staying at 18.
  • Later Underglow / ambient lighting — not against it, just last in line.
