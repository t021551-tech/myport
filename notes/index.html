How this works

Every log has the same shape: dated entries with a title, a rating, a few structured facts and two to four sentences. One template, six sets of content — which is why adding an entry takes about a minute, and why the site never looks finished. Each page ends with something still open: a wishlist, a “next to try”, a current best.
